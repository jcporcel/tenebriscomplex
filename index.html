About the Game

The HYPERCOMPLEX is all-encompassing. It stretches perpetually in all directions. An ever changing, chaotic amalgam of endless buildings, roads, pipes and mechanisms with no clear purpose.

Inside of its never-ending corridors and colossal halls, new iterations of humanity now survive in small settlements scattered across unfathomable distances fighting for what little resources are available against countless, nameless dangers.

The only masters of this realm are the god-like artificial intelligences that created it. The denizens of the Hypercomplex worship them, but the only law of the gods is their eternal unscrutable silence.
